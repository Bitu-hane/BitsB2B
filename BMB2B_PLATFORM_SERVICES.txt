================================================================================
                       BM B2B MARKETPLACE PLATFORM SERVICES
          Ethiopia's Wholesale & Enterprise Digital B2B E-Commerce Platform
================================================================================

1. VERIFIED ENTERPRISE IDENTITY & BUSINESS REGISTRATION
   - Digital registration for Importers, Manufacturers, Producers, Wholesalers, 
     Resellers, and Institutional Buyers in Ethiopia.
   - Formal trade license number and Tax Identification Number (TIN) verification.
   - Verified Supplier badges to ensure trust and filter out unverified traders.

2. WHOLESALE PRODUCT CATALOG & BULK TIERED PRICING
   - Multi-category wholesale sourcing across Industrial Machinery, Construction 
     Materials, Packaging & Printing, Agriculture, Automotive Parts, Chemicals, 
     Furniture, and Electronics.
   - Tiered volume pricing (discounted per-unit pricing based on order quantity).
   - Enforced Minimum Order Quantities (MOQ) and real-time stock availability status.

3. ESCROW TRADE ASSURANCE & SECURE PAYMENTS
   - Integrated digital payment gateways supporting Telebirr, CBE Birr, and Bank Transfers.
   - Escrow Protection: Buyer payments are securely held in escrow and only released 
     to the seller after the buyer inspects and confirms delivery.
   - Dispute management and refund request mechanism for damaged or non-conforming goods.

4. STRUCTURED RFQs & SUPPLIER INQUIRIES
   - Structured Request for Quotations (RFQs) for custom bulk order requirements.
   - Direct messaging tools for requesting physical product samples, technical 
     specifications, and lead-time negotiations.
   - Dedicated Inquiries Inbox for real-time buyer-seller communications.

5. SELLER & SUPPLIER MANAGEMENT DASHBOARD
   - Inventory management for listing products, stock levels, and technical specifications.
   - Order fulfillment workflow tracking (Order Placed -> Escrow Funded -> Dispatched -> Delivered -> Escrow Released).
   - Business performance metrics, response rates, and sales analytics.

6. BILINGUAL ENTERPRISE SUPPORT (AMHARIC & ENGLISH)
   - Complete localized interface in Amharic (አማርኛ) and English.
   - Persistent language preferences tailored for Ethiopian enterprises and local traders.

7. REAL-TIME NOTIFICATIONS & SMS ALERTS
   - Instant SMS notifications sent directly to business mobile numbers (+251).
   - In-app notification center for order milestones, escrow status changes, and new inquiry replies.
